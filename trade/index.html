Questions from the trade

Sourcing handmade Turkish rugs for a hotel, a showroom or a client's home? Here is how we work.

Who can apply for trade pricing?

Interior designers, architects, galleries, retailers and hospitality groups. Tell us about your business and your project, and we will reply within one business day with trade terms.

Do you supply wholesale lots of handmade rugs?

Yes. We assemble curated lots of hand-knotted silk and wool rugs to your sizes, palette and budget, including pieces from the archive that never reach the website.

Can you weave a custom rug to my size and design?

Yes. Send us the subject, palette and dimensions and we brief the master weavers we work with directly. A commission typically takes four to eight months on the loom, depending on size and knot density.

How are rugs shipped from Türkiye?

Every piece travels insured, rolled or framed, with export documentation, to anywhere in the world. Import duties depend on the destination country.

Can I see a rug before I buy?

We offer private viewings by video call, with close-ups of the pile, the back of the rug and the knots, so you can judge the weave for yourself.

Is every rug certified handmade?

Yes. Each piece is accompanied by a certificate of authenticity signed by the family and issued in the name of its new owner.
