Where the stone

Handmade in Türkiye

Turkish silk rugs,
from the loom to your room.

Professio offers hand-knotted silk rugs woven in Nevşehir, in the heart of Cappadocia, with the symmetrical Turkish knot and natural root dyes.

Our collection runs from small cabinet pieces and framed silks to Hereke-style medallion rugs, runners, round and octagonal rugs, and museum-scale pictorial carpets. Every rug is one of one and leaves us with a signed certificate of authenticity and insured worldwide delivery.

New to silk? Our silk rug buying guide explains how to tell real silk from art silk, what knot density means and what drives the price. Collectors who want the history can read about Hereke silk, Kayseri and Anatolian weaving and the Persian tradition. Interior designers, hotels and retailers can source through our wholesale and trade programme.

Certified Handmade

Signed by the family.

Every Professio rug leaves us with a certificate affirming it is handmade, issued in the name of its new custodian.
