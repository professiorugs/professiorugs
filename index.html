Heritage & Origins

Professio offers hand-knotted silk floor rugs from Hereke and Kayseri, the great silk-weaving towns of Türkiye, and pictorial wall carpets from the workshops of Tabriz in Iran.

Every floor rug is tied by hand with the symmetrical Turkish knot. The collection runs from small cabinet pieces to Hereke medallion rugs, runners, round and octagonal rugs, and museum-scale Tabriz wall carpets. Every rug is one of one and leaves us with a signed certificate of authenticity and insured worldwide delivery.

New to silk? Our silk rug buying guide explains how to tell real silk from art silk, what knot density means and what drives the price. Collectors who want the history can read about Hereke silk, Kayseri and Anatolian weaving and the Persian tradition. Interior designers, hotels and retailers can source through our wholesale and trade programme.

Kayseri · Cappadocia

Where the stone
keeps the silk.

Kayseri, the old capital of Cappadocia, has knotted silk since the caravans carried it along the roads past its walls. It is a land of fairy chimneys and rock-cut chapels, shaped by wind and by centuries of patient hands. The light there falls slowly, honey by day and indigo by night. You can see both in our Kayseri silks.
