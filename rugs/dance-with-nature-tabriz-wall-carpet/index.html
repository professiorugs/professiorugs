The Soul of the Piece

Some rugs are woven; this one was kept alive. Strand by strand of silk, a garden was coaxed into being: musicians who never tire, dancers caught between one step and the next, a river that has been running for as long as the loom was strung. They say a carpet remembers every hand that touched it. Silk is the same. Stand close in the evening, when the lamps are low, and the whole scene seems to breathe, as if the party has only paused for you to arrive. A piece like this is not finished when it leaves the loom. It is finished when it finds the room that deserves it.
