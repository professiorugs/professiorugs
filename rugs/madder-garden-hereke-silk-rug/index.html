The Soul of the Piece

Madder is one of the oldest reds known to weavers, drawn from a humble root that hides its colour underground. Here it becomes a whole field, and on it a scattering of blossoms in blue, rose and cream, as if a garden had been carried in from outside and laid down to rest. Warm, generous, impossible to ignore.
