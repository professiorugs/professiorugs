The Soul of the Piece

Imagine a palace corridor above the Sea of Marmara, a row of niches each holding a single vessel of flowers, each lit by its own small flame. That is the feeling of this rug. Beneath every arch a vase stands in quiet devotion, and the midnight border closes around them like the dark beyond the lamplight. The colours came from roots and leaves, steeped and waited on with a patience modern life has forgotten. It is a piece for the hour after dinner, when conversation slows and the silk begins to glow.
