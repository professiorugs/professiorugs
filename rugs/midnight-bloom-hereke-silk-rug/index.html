The Soul of the Piece

Night falls fast over the Gulf of İzmit, and the water below Hereke turns from silver to indigo in minutes. This rug lives in that hour. Pale blossoms open across a deep navy field, and the crimson border holds them like the last warmth of the day. It asks for soft light and rewards it.
